1) About the iSACCO Member App

The iSACCO Member App is a mobile application that enables members of participating Savings and Credit Cooperative Organizations (SACCOs) to access SACCO services digitally. Depending on your SACCO’s configuration, the app may allow you to:

  • Sign in securely and view your member profile
  • View balances for savings/deposits, share capital, and other SACCO products
  • View loan information, apply for loans, and track loan status
  • Access statements and transaction history
  • Initiate payments or contributions where supported (for example, via mobile money integrations)
  • Receive service notifications (for example: loan updates, payment status, system messages)
Important
The app is an access channel to your SACCO’s services. Your SACCO remains responsible for SACCO membership rules, approvals, pricing (interest/fees), and account administration under its bylaws and policies.

3) Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 18, 2026

3.1 Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the iSACCO Member App collects, uses, shares, and protects your information. The App is used to access SACCO services. Your SACCO may also have its own privacy and data handling policies.

3.2 Information We Collect

A) Information you provide

  • Identity and profile details: name, member number, phone number, email (where applicable)
  • Login credentials (stored securely and/or tokenized)
  • Requests and submissions: loan applications, support tickets, uploaded documents (if enabled)

B) Information generated through use

  • Account and SACCO data displayed in the app: savings/deposits, share capital, loans, statements, transactions
  • Device and app diagnostics: app version, device model, OS version, crash logs (where enabled)
  • Security and audit information: login timestamps, IP address (where available), device identifiers (for fraud prevention)

C) Payment information (if enabled)

  • Transaction references, amounts, timestamps, status updates returned by payment providers
  • We do not intentionally store full payment card data. Mobile money data is handled via the payment provider and status callbacks.

3.3 How We Use Information

  • To authenticate you and provide access to SACCO services
  • To display your SACCO account information and transaction history
  • To process requests (e.g., loan applications) and route them to Your SACCO
  • To prevent fraud, detect abuse, and protect accounts
  • To maintain and improve the App, fix bugs, and ensure reliability
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests

3.4 Legal Bases (where applicable)

We process data based on one or more of the following: providing the service you request, legitimate interests in security and fraud prevention, compliance with legal obligations, and consent where required (for example, optional marketing or notifications).

3.5 Sharing of Information

We may share information with:

  • Your SACCO: to provide SACCO services, handle approvals, manage accounts, and respond to inquiries.
  • Service providers: hosting, analytics/crash reporting (if enabled), SMS/email delivery, and support tools, under contractual confidentiality and security obligations.
  • Payment providers (if enabled): to initiate and confirm transactions. We receive transaction statuses from these providers.
  • Legal and regulatory authorities: where required by law or valid legal process.
No selling of personal data
We do not sell your personal data.

3.6 Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide the service, meet SACCO operational requirements, and comply with legal and regulatory obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on data type (e.g., transaction and audit logs may be retained longer for compliance).

3.7 Security

We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, which may include:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for data communications
  • Secure token-based authentication
  • Access controls, audit logs, and least-privilege principles
  • Monitoring and security updates

No system is 100% secure. You should also protect your device, keep your OS updated, and never share your credentials.

3.8 Your Rights & Choices

Subject to applicable law and SACCO requirements, you may have rights to:

  • Access and correct your personal data
  • Request deletion (where applicable and not legally restricted)
  • Object to certain processing or request restriction
  • Withdraw consent for optional features

To exercise these rights, use the Support contact or your SACCO office.

3.9 Children’s Privacy

The App is generally intended for adult SACCO members. Where a SACCO supports junior accounts or guardianship features, access and processing may be subject to SACCO policy, guardian authorization, and applicable law.

3.10 International Transfers

If hosting or service providers are located outside your country, data may be transferred and processed in other jurisdictions. We require reasonable safeguards and contractual protections where applicable.

3.11 Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the updated policy.
