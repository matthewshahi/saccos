About Us

Mission: To empower our members by providing reliable and personalized financial solutions that promote economic stability and growth.

Vision: To be the leading SACCO known for financial excellence, transparency, and community growth.

Core Values: Integrity, Service, Transparency, Accountability, and Innovation.

Our Story: Established in January 2007 by staff from iWayAfrica Services in Kenya, iSave SACCO has grown to serve over 130 members. We are committed to promoting financial independence and supporting our community's economic development.

Membership

How to Join: Fill out the membership form, provide the required documents, and submit them to our office or through our online portal.

Membership Benefits: Access to affordable loans, exclusive investment opportunities, financial advisory services, and educational resources.

Eligibility Criteria: Open to all individuals interested in joining a supportive financial community.
