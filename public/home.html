About Us

iSave SACCO was formed in January 2007 by a group of staff from iWayAfrica Services in Kenya. It currently has a membership of over 130 members. Our mission is to empower our members by providing reliable and personalized financial solutions. We believe in community growth, transparency, and supporting our members on their financial journeys.
